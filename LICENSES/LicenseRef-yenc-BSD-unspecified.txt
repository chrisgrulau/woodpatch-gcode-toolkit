yenc.js (legacy/webapp/libs/yenc.js)

A modified copy of yEnc by Weston Houghton (https://github.com/whoughton/yEnc),
matching that project's version 0.9.0 (commit 1ab6046, 2013-05-30).

That project declared its licence only as "license": "BSD" in package.json. It
shipped no licence text and did not say which BSD variant it meant, so no exact
SPDX identifier can be assigned. It is redistributed here as received from
upstream webgcode, under whatever BSD terms its author intended.

This file is parked in legacy/ and is never built, bundled, published or served.
Nothing may be copied from it into the toolkit's packages.
