Third-party libraries vendored by upstream webgcode (legacy/webapp/libs/**).

These files were bundled into nraynaud/webgcode by its author. They are
redistributed here exactly as received from upstream, as part of the fork's
history and its parked legacy/ tree. Each file is the work of its own authors
and remains under its own licence. Where a file carries a licence header, that
header is authoritative.

The per-library licences have not yet been recorded individually. That audit is
scheduled before Phase 1 relies on anything in this directory beyond
libs/jsparse.js. It will replace this identifier with real SPDX identifiers per
library in REUSE.toml (docs/DECISIONS.md, ADR-0005).

Nothing here is built, bundled, published as a package or served by this
project. The Phase 1 characterisation harness (tools/legacy-harness.cjs) loads
libs/jsparse.js (Copyright (C) 2007 Chris Double, BSD-style licence per its
header) at test time only.

Do not copy code out of this directory into the toolkit's own packages without
first establishing that file's licence.
