Third-party libraries vendored by upstream webgcode (legacy/webapp/libs/**).

These files were bundled into nraynaud/webgcode by its author and arrived with
the fork's history. Each is the work of its own authors and is under its own
licence; where a file carries a licence header, that header is authoritative.
They have NOT been individually audited by this project.

They are parked, not used: nothing under legacy/ is built, bundled, published
or served by any package in this repository, and legacy/ is slated for deletion
after one release (docs/DECISIONS.md, ADR-0005). Git history and the tag
upstream-2025-09-18 preserve them regardless.

Do not import from, copy out of, or redistribute anything in legacy/webapp/libs/
without first establishing that file's actual licence. The one planned exception
is the Phase 1 characterisation harness (tools/legacy-harness.js), which loads
libs/jsparse.js (Copyright (C) 2007 Chris Double, BSD-style licence per its
header) at test time only, and never ships it.
