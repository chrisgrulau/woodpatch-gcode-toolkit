Interpreter (`interpret`): modal state, RS274 order of execution, units, planes, work offsets
(G10/G92/G52/G53/G28/G30) in LinuxCNC's parameter layout, arcs as descriptions, and spindle,
coolant, tool, dwell, pause and program-end steps. Lines that can't run are reported and skipped.
